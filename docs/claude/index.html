Use Domain Hound in Claude

Check whether a domain is available, compare its first-year and renewal prices at six shops, and see if an app, public profile or exact US trademark already uses the name.

The connector is read-only. Any purchase or registration happens at the registrar.

Add the connector

  1. In Claude, open Settings > Connectors > Add custom connector. If your app shows Customize, open Customize > Connectors > Add > Custom > Web.
  2. Name the connector Domain Hound.
  3. Paste this URL and continue.
https://domainhound-mcp.domainhound.workers.dev/mcp

Choose No sign in for authentication and finish adding the connector. Enable Domain Hound from the + menu under Connectors in your chat.

If you cannot add it, check whether your organization allows custom connectors. See Claude’s setup help.

Try these prompts

“Use Domain Hound to check if example.com is available.”
The status of that exact domain. The result says whether it is taken, available or could not be confirmed.
“Compare .com, .dev and .app for Fern Quartz Studio. Show three-year costs and any exact US trademark matches.”
The three endings, published prices for available domains, a pick if one fits the budget, and name-check results. A trademark match includes a link when a record is available.
“Check the US App Store for an app named Fern Quartz Studio.”
Matching US App Store titles and links, or a message that no match was found or the check could not finish.

Trademark results cover exact live US word marks. Search aid, not legal advice.

Where the results come from

Domain Hound checks public registry and DNS records. It compares published list prices from Spaceship, Porkbun, Namecheap, Dynadot, Cloudflare and Gandi where they list the ending. A shop can quote a different price at checkout.

Name checks use the US Patent and Trademark Office, Apple’s US App Store catalog, Google Play, and public profile pages, embeds and archives. If a source cannot answer, the result says so.

Claude sends the names you ask to check to Domain Hound. See the privacy policy.
