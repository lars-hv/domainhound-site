Privacy

Who we are

Domain Hound is made by BrightBoard AS, org. no. 935 969 034, Middelthuns gate 13C, 0368 Oslo, Norway. We are responsible for the personal data described here. For privacy questions and requests, write to privacy@domainhound.dev.

The tool on your computer

Domain Hound runs on your own computer as an MCP server. We run no search server, so we never receive the names you check or your results. The tool uses no analytics, telemetry or cookies.

Who sees the names you check?

Your computer contacts public sources directly. Each service can see your IP address. If a check sends a name, the service sees that name too. Each service’s own privacy policy applies.

  • Domains and prices. Domain Hound checks domain registries through RDAP or WHOIS. It also checks DNS records and reads price lists from Spaceship, Porkbun, Dynadot and Gandi.
  • Trademarks and companies. It checks the USPTO trademark search, Norway’s Brønnøysund registers and company records from SEC EDGAR.
  • Apps, projects and handles. It looks for names on the Apple App Store, Google Play, GitHub, npm, PyPI, X and YouTube.
  • Names on the web. It can check Google autocomplete, Google Trends and Wikidata. It may visit the domain itself and check Tranco and Common Crawl for signs of use. If you set up Brave Search or Serper, it can send your search there too.

Domain Hound may save results on your computer so it can reuse them the next time you check.

Your AI assistant

Claude, Codex or whichever assistant you use sees the results Domain Hound sends back. The assistant provider’s privacy policy applies to those results.

Live price checks

If you ask for a live price, Domain Hound opens the shop’s public search page in a hidden browser on your computer. The shop sees the domain you check. Domain Hound does this without signing in or buying anything.

Domain watch alerts

  • We store your email address, your name if you give it, the domains you ask us to watch, and when you signed up and confirmed.
  • You can sign up on domainhound.dev or ask your AI assistant, such as Claude or Codex, to sign you up. Your assistant sends us what you ask it to send. Either way, your watch starts when you click the confirmation link we email you.
  • We send a confirmation email. After that, we email you when a domain you watch expires, when it is about to be released, and when it is free. We send at most four watch emails per domain, including confirmation.
  • We use your data to provide the watch you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • We delete unconfirmed sign-ups after 7 days. We remove watches when they end or when you erase them. A backup copy can remain for up to 30 days. Every watch email has a link to see and stop your watches and a one-click link to erase your address, watches and news subscription.
  • We do not track opens or clicks in our emails.

Domain Hound news (optional)

  • If you say yes, we email you news about Domain Hound and other developer tools from BrightBoard AS. At most twice a month.
  • You can tick the empty box on our site and then confirm your watch by email. You can also choose “Confirm watch + news” in your confirmation email. If your AI assistant asks about news, your answer only chooses which button we highlight. Your click in the email is what counts. Saying no does not affect your watches.
  • We send news with your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the Norwegian Marketing Control Act section 15).
  • We keep a record of when you said yes, where you signed up (site or assistant) and the exact words you agreed to.
  • Every news email has our postal address and a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also write to us. We stop at once, at the latest within 10 days.
  • When you unsubscribe from news or erase your data, we keep an address hash on our do-not-send list. This lets us check whether an address should receive news without storing the email address itself on that list (our legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep the record of your consent for 3 years after you withdraw it to show what you agreed to. After erasure, that record contains only the address hash, the version of the consent text and the dates and times.
  • If we ever want to send a wider kind of email, we will ask you again.

Who helps us

  • Cloudflare runs the alert service and stores our records under a data processing agreement that covers US transfers through the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and uses EU standard contract clauses where needed.
  • Resend sends our emails through Amazon SES in Ireland and stores message data in the US under EU standard contract clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • GitHub independently hosts this free Pages site, logs visitor IP addresses for security, and describes EU standard contract clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework in its privacy policy for transfers to the US.

We never sell your data or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Children

Domain Hound is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly store their data.

No automated decisions

We make no decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similar effects.

Changes

You can ask to see, correct, export or erase your data, and object to or limit how we use it. Write to privacy@domainhound.dev. You can also complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.
