FAQ

Can two iOS apps share a name?
Apple says, “You can use an app name for one app per localization.” Try the name in App Store Connect.
Is the Android title the app’s ID?
No. Google Play uses a unique, permanent package name. The title shoppers see is a separate field.
What if both searches are empty?
Check similar trademarks, domains and handles. Store searches miss unpublished apps and other countries.
