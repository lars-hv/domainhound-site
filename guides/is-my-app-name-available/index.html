FAQ

Can I claim the iOS title?
Try it in App Store Connect. Apple allows one app to use a given name in each localization.
Is the Android title the app’s ID?
Google Play uses a unique, permanent package name. The title shoppers see is a separate field.
What if both searches are empty?
Check similar trademarks, domains and handles. Store searches miss unpublished apps and names outside the storefront searched.
