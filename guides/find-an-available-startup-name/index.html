How to find an available startup name

Brindle Ledger passed. Moss Loom failed two checks on November 25, 2026. We wanted the exact .com and X handle for a bookkeeping app.

What the hound checks

Domains
Check the registry, then compare the first payment and the renewal. Try .com, .ai, .io, .co, .app, .dev, .net, .org, .xyz, .tech, .me, .so, .si and .no. Country options include .co.uk and .eu.
Apps
Look for the name in the App Store and Google Play.
Google
See which names turn up in search and autocomplete. The agent also checks site rankings, pages found by web crawlers and the website on the domain.
Trademarks and companies
Screen US marks through the USPTO. Check EUIPO in the EU and Patentstyret in Norway with a free key or a search link. Company checks cover the Norwegian company register and US SEC records.
Meaning
Check for rude words and words that sound like them in 14 languages.
Handles
Check LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and GitHub. Open “Check” to look for a profile yourself. The agent also checks npm and Bluesky and links to Facebook.

FAQ

How do I share a shortlist?
Select “Share shortlist” and send the link to your cofounder.
Should I start with the logo?
Start with the shortlist. Changing a word on a page is cheaper than changing a sign.
Do I need every handle?
Only where your buyers look for you. Check who holds each handle and what they sell.
What comes before registration?
Check the renewal price, search similar marks in each market and read the shop’s final quote.
