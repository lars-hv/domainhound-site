FAQ

Should I start with the logo?
Start with the shortlist. Changing a word on a page is cheaper than changing a sign.
Do I need every handle?
Check the places where buyers will look for you. Look at who uses each handle and what they sell.
What comes before registration?
Check renewal, search similar marks in your markets and read the shop’s final quote.
