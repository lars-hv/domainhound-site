How Domain Hound does it

Ask for the same name under each ending. You get the availability and both prices together. At Spaceship, .ai requires 2 years at $79.98 per year. The first payment is $159.96 before tax.

FAQ

Are these my odds of finding a name?
No. The sample uses 200 English names we picked. It shows how those names fared on one day.
What does .dev add?
It signals a developer audience. Say the full address aloud and see whether people remember the ending.
What if someone owns the .com?
Visit it. An active business with a similar product is a reason to try another name.
