Should you buy .com, .ai or .io?

Start with .com if customers will guess your address from the name. If it is taken, weigh the cost of a new name against the renewal bill for .ai, .io or .dev.

How Domain Hound does it

Ask for the same name under each ending. Read the registry result and renewal together. A free .ai might fit a product well, but you need enough cash for its two year term.

FAQ

Are these my odds of finding a name?
No. The sample uses English roots and endings we picked. It shows how those particular names fared.
What does .dev add?
It signals a developer audience. Say the full address aloud and see whether people remember the ending.
What if someone owns the .com?
Visit it. An active business with a similar product is a reason to try another name.
