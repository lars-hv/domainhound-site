FAQ

What does “no close hit” tell me?
Only that this US screen returned no close mark. A related mark, an unregistered business or a name abroad may still matter.
What should I do with a hit?
Read the full mark record. Compare the sound, meaning, goods and buyers. A trademark lawyer can assess a close call.
Does owning the domain give me rights to the mark?
Buying a web address does not settle who may trade under the name.
