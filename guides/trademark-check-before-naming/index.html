FAQ

What does “no close hit” tell me?
Only that this US screen returned no close mark. A related mark, an unregistered business or a mark abroad can still matter.
What should I do with a hit?
Open the full record. Compare sound, meaning, goods and buyers. A trademark lawyer can judge a close call.
Does owning the domain give me rights to the mark?
No. Buying a web address does not settle who may trade under the name.
