FAQ

How short should the name be?
Try saying it over the phone. A longer word people know can be easier to spell than a short word you invented.
Can I just leave out a vowel?
Try it on someone who has never seen the word. If you have to spell it every time, keep looking.
What if my favorite name is free?
Look at the search results first. An empty registry record tells you little about the brands your customers already know.
