How Domain Hound does it

Give the assistant a product idea and it gets a naming recipe. Send a shortlist and Domain Hound checks domains, renewal prices, apps, US marks, Google results and handles.

Install in Claude Code, or use Codex.

claude mcp add domain-hound -- npx -y domain-hound

FAQ

Which one generates names?
Domain Hound gives your assistant a naming recipe. The assistant writes the shortlist, then Domain Hound checks it.
Are Domain Hound prices binding?
No. They are dated list prices. The shop’s checkout has the final price, and premium names cost more.
Will it clear a trademark or buy a domain?
Neither. It screens US marks. You handle clearance and checkout.
