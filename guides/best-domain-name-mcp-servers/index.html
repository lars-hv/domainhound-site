FAQ

Which one generates names?
Domain Hound gives your assistant a naming recipe. The assistant writes the shortlist.
Are Domain Hound prices binding?
Most are dated list prices. A confirmed quote needs a shop response, and premium prices can differ.
Will it clear a trademark or buy a domain?
It screens US marks. You handle clearance and checkout.
