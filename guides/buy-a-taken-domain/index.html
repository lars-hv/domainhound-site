How to buy a taken domain

ICANN lists 30 days for redemption (checked 2026-11-25). To buy sooner, look for a sale listing or contact the owner through the registrar.

What happens after a .com expiresChecked 2026-11-25 UTC
StageDaysWho can act
Auto-renew grace0 to 45The registrar sets its renewal deadline and can delete the name.
Redemption grace30The previous owner can ask the registrar to restore it.
Pending delete5Restoration closes. The registry releases the name after this stage.

ICANN’s timeline covers typical generic endings. Redemption starts when the registrar deletes the name. A registrar may renew or auction it instead. On November 25, Verisign listed October 9, 2028 as the expiry of github.com. That date can change if the owner renews.

FAQ

Does a domain drop at expiry?
No. The registrar may still allow renewal. Check its deadline. Redemption begins only if the registrar deletes the name.
What does pending delete mean?
The last 5 days. Nobody can restore the name. Then the registry releases it, and the first registration wins.
What should I pay?
Set your ceiling before you contact the owner. Agree the transfer in writing and use an escrow service you trust.
