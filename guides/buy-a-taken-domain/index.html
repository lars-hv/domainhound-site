FAQ

Does a domain drop at expiry?
Usually there are more steps. For many generic endings, the registrar can still renew or auction the name. After deletion, redemption gives the prior owner a chance to restore it.
What does pending delete mean?
After redemption ends without restoration, the registry can place the name in its final pending delete stage. Registry rules govern release. Other buyers can reach it first.
What should I pay?
Set your ceiling before contacting the owner. Verify control, agree on the transfer in writing and use an escrow service you trust. A registry result gives no resale valuation.
