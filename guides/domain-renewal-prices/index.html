How Domain Hound does it

Ask for both payments on an exact domain. Domain Hound compares dated public price lists. These are standard rates before tax. Premium names, coupons and resale deals can change the bill.

FAQ

How much is due for .ai?
The listed rate is per year. A two year term at the table rate comes to $159.96 up front.
Does the cheapest first year win?
No. Dynadot listed .io at $21.40 for the first year and $53.50 to renew. Spaceship charged more up front and less at renewal.
Can anyone buy a .no?
Norid has eligibility rules, including a Norwegian postal address. Check the rules before you choose it.
