FAQ

How much is due for .ai?
Spaceship sells .ai for 2 years minimum at $79.98 a year. That is $159.96 up front.
Does the cheapest first year win?
No. Dynadot listed .io at $21.40 for the first year and $53.50 to renew. Spaceship charged more up front and less at renewal.
Can anyone buy a .no?
No. Norid’s policy, section 5, allows people over 18 in the Norwegian population register with a Norwegian national ID, and eligible registered organisations. Both need a local postal address.
