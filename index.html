What emails will I get?

Up to four per watch, including confirmation. We’ll email if the registry reports expiry or pending deletion (about five days left), and when we find the domain free to register. No scheduled digests.

News is optional. Each watch email has a private manage link. Turn off news, stop one watch or erase your address and watches.
