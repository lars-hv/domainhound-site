    How we counted

    Snapshot dated November 27, 2026. We counted one listed website per company across 13 YC batches, from Winter 2023 through Fall 2026. 10 of 1,591 eligible companies had no usable website. A website can change after launch.

    Coverage, sources and terms

    We matched any listed California location to a Bay Area city or town, plus Stanford. Companies with multiple locations count once. Remote-only and future batches are excluded. This sample covers publicly launched YC companies across all statuses.

    We used registry endings from the Public Suffix List so endings like .co.uk stay together. Lengths count the label immediately before that ending. For brand matches and prefixes, we compare lowercase letters and digits and ignore punctuation and spaces. Legal endings such as Inc. stay in the listed brand.

    For name shapes, WordNet supplies English words. Compounds have dictionary parts separated by spaces, hyphens, capitalization or a split into two words of at least three letters each. Unmatched single alphabetic tokens are possibly made-up. Other spellings stay unclassified.

    Our source is YC OSS, an unofficial feed of the YC directory. YC’s terms of use restrict scraping and commercial reuse of site content. The feed does not grant a separate data license. Domain Hound is independent of YC.
