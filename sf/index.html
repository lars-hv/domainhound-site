Matching rule, data and sources

We compare the full listed name with the label immediately before the registry ending. We ignore case, spaces and punctuation, and keep words such as “AI” and “Inc.” in the brand. For example, a brand listed as “Example.ai” differs from the label “example” under this rule.

Each company with a listed Bay Area city or town, or Stanford, counts once. Remote-only entries are excluded. The charts describe current addresses and cannot tell us why a founder chose a domain or how the company performed.
